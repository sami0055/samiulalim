🧑 Career Summary

I am a machine learning researcher and hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (Minor in Mathematics, Cum Laude) from North South University, Dhaka. Over the past three years I have built a research record across vision–language and multimodal learning, efficient deep learning, NLP for social science, and AI for healthcare, with six peer-reviewed publications including three Q1 journal articles and an accepted paper at AACL 2026.

My research centres on a single goal: advancing trustworthy AI at the intersection of language, vision, and society, with healthcare and low-resource, socially sensitive settings as primary application domains. I pursue this across three interconnected directions:

  1. Vision–Language and Multimodal Learning: cross-modal alignment, modality balancing, tokenizer-free byte-level captioning, and the robustness and test-time adaptation of vision–language foundation models.
  2. Efficient and Trustworthy AI: multi-teacher knowledge distillation, context-aware one-shot pruning, and model compression for resource-constrained hardware, together with calibration, uncertainty, and interpretability of deep models.
  3. AI for Healthcare and Society: explainable diagnostic modelling on medical images and neuroimaging, large-scale sentiment and discourse analysis of social media during crises, LLM evaluation on politically sensitive text, and Responsible AI with human-centred evaluation.

I am currently a Summer Research Intern at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC++ SRSE 2026), working on Responsible AI and Human–Computer Interaction with Dr. Sharifa Sultana. I collaborate with Prof. Mohammad Abu Yousuf (Jahangirnagar University) and Dr. Mohammad Ali Moni (The University of Queensland) on explainable medical AI and crisis NLP, with Prof. Pietro Liò (University of Cambridge) on test-time adaptation of vision–language models, and with the Machine Intelligence Lab at North South University on multimodal learning and model compression, where I led CLIPByte, a tokenizer-free multilingual captioning model.

My work has appeared in Knowledge-Based Systems (Q1, IF 8.0), Neurocomputing (Q1, IF 6.7), PLOS ONE (Q1), AACL 2026, IEEE BigData 2025 (CORE B), and IEEE ICCIT 2025, with further manuscripts under review at ICLR 2027 (A*) and WACV 2026 (A). I review for Knowledge-Based Systems, Array, and PLOS ONE, spent two years as an undergraduate teaching assistant across four mathematics courses, and volunteer as a science communicator with Biggani.org.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027. Feel free to email me (samiul.alim01 at northsouth dot edu) or connect via the links below if you are interested in collaboration or discussing research.

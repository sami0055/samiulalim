  • Research Assistant, Machine Intelligence Lab, North South University | Dhaka, Bangladesh | December 2025 – June 2026

    • Lead author on CLIPByte, a tokenizer-free vision–language model for multilingual open-vocabulary image captioning across six typologically diverse languages, aligning a CLIP ViT-B/32 visual encoder with a ByT5-small byte-level decoder to remove tokenizer-induced out-of-vocabulary bottlenecks, and built its multilingual evaluation pipeline (CIDEr, METEOR, ROUGE-L, BLEU).
